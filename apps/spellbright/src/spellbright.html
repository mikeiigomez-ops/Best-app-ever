How to play

A storm knocked every letter off Lumen City's neon signs. Spell words in levels to earn stars, then spend them on Main Street to relight the signs.

  1. Drag through touching letters to spell a word of 3 to 8 letters. Diagonals count, and each tile can be used once per word.
    STAR
  2. The word clears and new letters drop in. Each word uses one move. If it isn't a word, nothing happens and no move is used.
  3. Longer words score far more, and they leave a special tile where they end: 5 letters a ×2 tile, 6 letters a bomb that blows up the 3×3 around it when you use it, and 7 or more a ★ wild that stands for any letter.
    ET★
  4. Play words of 4+ letters in a row to build a combo, up to ×3. A 3-letter word resets it.
  5. Every letter you clear charges the power meter. Each time it fills you earn a powerup. Using one costs no move: Shuffle mixes up the board, Line clear wipes out a row or column, Bomb clears a 3×3 square, and Wild turns a tile into a ★. Unused powerups are worth 1,500 points each at the end.
  6. Levels: beat the target score for a star, and the higher marks for two and three. Daily: everyone gets the same board and 20 words.
