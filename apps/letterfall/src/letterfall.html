Swap two neighbouring letters to spell a word across or down. Drag a tile, or tap one and then its neighbour.

How to play

  1. Swap two neighbouring letters to spell a word of 3 to 6 letters, reading left to right or top to bottom.
    STAR
  2. Words clear and the letters above fall. If the fall spells more words, they score again with a chain bonus.
  3. Longer words score far more. A 5-letter word leaves a ×2 tile and a 6-letter word a ×3. Use them in a word to multiply it, and two together multiply each other.
    EA
  4. When an across word and a down word share a letter, that's a crossing: both score double and your power meter jumps.
  5. Every letter you clear charges the power meter. Each time it fills you earn a powerup. Using one costs no move: Shuffle mixes up the board, Line clear wipes out a row or column, Bomb clears a 3×3 square, and Wild turns a tile into a ★ that stands for any letter.
  6. A swap that spells nothing bounces back and costs nothing. Powerups you haven't used when the moves run out are worth 1,500 points each.
  7. Levels: beat the target score for a star, and hit the higher marks for two and three. Each level is a little tougher than the last.
  8. Daily: everyone gets the same board and 20 moves. Share your score.
